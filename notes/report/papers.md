### Summary

1. **Security & Adversarial Guardrails (Papers 1, 7, 11, 12, 13, 18, 21):**
   * Guides the multi-surface defense strategy across input validation (pre-retrieval), ingestion quarantine (zero database contamination), and stream output redaction (zero secret leakage).
2. **Retrieval, Ranking & Context Engineering (Papers 2, 3, 8, 10, 15, 16, 17, 20):**
   * Informs the parent-child indexing model, dense/lexical hybrid search, cross-encoder reranking, and perimeter context placement to neutralize "Lost in the Middle" degradation.
3. **Generation, Grounding & Self-Correction (Papers 4, 5, 6, 9, 19):**
   * Dictates the fallback and refusal mechanisms when context is insufficient, claim-level faithfulness verification, and 1:1 citation binding with ghost footnote elimination.
4. **Evaluation & Quality Assurance (Papers 14, 18):**
   * Establishes the mathematical metrics ($\text{ADR}$, $\text{DCR}$, $\text{SLR}$, Faithfulness, Context Recall, Context Precision) driving automated CI testing in `tests/evals/test_golden_dataset.py`.
